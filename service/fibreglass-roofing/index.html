Fibreglass Roofing

Fibreglass Roofing, done properly.

GRP fibreglass roofs are laid wet and cure into one seamless, rigid surface, which makes them a strong choice for flat roofs, balconies and dormers. Roofers 365 installs fibreglass roofs with neat trims and a durable top coat.

Fibreglass handles foot traffic well and has a clean, modern look. We make sure the deck, falls and edges are right before we lay it.

Common questions

Fibreglass Roofing: your questions answered.

Is fibreglass good for a flat roof?

Yes. GRP is seamless, strong and long-lasting when installed in suitable conditions on a sound deck.

Can fibreglass be walked on?

Yes, a properly installed GRP roof handles light foot traffic, which is why it is popular for balconies.

What colours are available?

Top coats come in a range of colours, with grey and charcoal the most common.
